Case Closed ("the App"), developed and operated by Appibara LTD ("we", "us", or "our"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information automatically collected when you use the App.

### A. Personal Data

When you register or use the App, we may collect:

- **Authentication Data:** Name, email address, and profile picture provided through third-party authentication services (Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In), or an automatically generated anonymous user identifier (Firebase Authentication UID) if you play as a guest.
- **Contact Information:** Email address for customer support, technical assistance, and account-related communications.

### B. Device and Usage Information

To ensure the App functions correctly, we automatically collect:

- **Device Identifiers:** Unique user ID (Firebase UID) and IP address.
- **Technical Data:** Device model, operating system version, and system language.
- **Usage Logs:** Solved mystery cases, case dossiers opened, suspect answers submitted, interaction timestamps, and crash reports.

### C. Local Storage

We use local storage technologies (such as MMKV and Keychain) to store your authentication session, game progress, and user preferences on your device. This data is used to keep you signed in, preserve your detective dossier, and maintain your settings.

### D. Purchase and Subscription Data

The App offers consumable virtual currency ("Tickets") and auto-renewable subscriptions ("Detective Membership" / "VIP").
- If you purchase tickets or subscriptions, we receive transactional metadata (subscription status, product ID, transaction timestamps, and expiration details) via **RevenueCat**.
- All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store). We **never** collect, store, or have access to your credit card or billing information.

### E. Advertising Information

The App displays rewarded video advertisements (allowing you to earn free virtual tickets) and occasional banner ads via **Google AdMob**. We deploy Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage your privacy and consent choices.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the App (unlocking cases, viewing evidence, and submitting answers).
- Manage your user account, verify your identity, and merge anonymous guest progress when you link Apple or Google accounts.
- Process in-app purchases and credit virtual tickets to your detective dossier.
- Serve rewarded video ads and contextual advertisements based on your consent preferences.
- Analyze gameplay patterns and improve App performance.
- Detect, prevent, and address technical glitches, unauthorized tampering, or fraud.
- Comply with legal obligations.

**We do not sell your personal data to third parties.**

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services. If an anonymous account remains inactive for more than twelve (12) consecutive months, associated progress data may be removed from our servers.

### Account and Data Deletion

You have the permanent right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time:

- **In-App:** Open the App, navigate to **Profile**, and select **"DELETE ACCOUNT"**.
- **Request via Email:** You may also request data deletion by contacting us at [contact@appibara.com](mailto:contact@appibara.com) with the subject *"Account Deletion Request"*. Upon deletion, your personal data, solved cases, and virtual ticket balances will be permanently removed from our active databases, subject to legal retention requirements.
